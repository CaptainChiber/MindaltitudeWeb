Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

English

MindAltitude is designed to provide aviation mental training exercises for pilots and student pilots.

Data Collection

MindAltitude does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

  • No user account is required.
  • No advertising is used.
  • No tracking is performed.
  • No analytics data is collected.
  • No location, health or sensitive data is collected.
  • No information is shared with third parties.

If Premium features are offered, purchases are processed exclusively through Apple’s App Store using Apple’s secure payment systems. The developer does not receive or store payment information.
